## 1. Product Overview

## Project Name

GUO YUSHENG --- DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Internal concept:

G-CORE

## Product Type

Immersive WebGL Personal Digital Experience.

This is not a traditional portfolio website.

The website itself is the first showcase of technical ability.

The goal is to create a digital exhibition space where visitors explore
the creator's identity, projects, technologies, and ideas through an
interactive 3D environment.

# 5. Experience Concept

The website is designed as a digital universe.

The visitor enters a virtual laboratory.

The creator's identity is represented by a 3D G Core object.

Projects, technologies, and experiments exist around this core.

The experience is divided into several scenes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Scene Overview

## Scene 01

## Identity Core

Purpose:

Introduce the creator identity.

Experience:

A dark digital space initializes.

A giant G object is generated through light, particles, and material
effects.

The visitor discovers:

GUO YUSHENG

Main technologies:

-   Three.js
-   React Three Fiber
-   GSAP
-   Shader effects

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scene 02

## Project Archive

Purpose:

Showcase projects as digital artifacts.

Concept:

Projects are not cards.

They are objects existing in a 3D archive.

Experience:

The G Core transitions into particles.

Particles enter a deep digital space.

The camera moves downward.

Project objects appear gradually.

Interaction:

-   Mouse controls rotation
-   Camera explores the archive
-   Projects become dominant when approaching the viewer

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scene 03

## Technology Matrix

Purpose:

Show technical ecosystem.

    GSAP 85%

Instead:

Create a spatial technology system.

Nodes:

-   React
-   GSAP
-   Three.js

-   AI Tools
